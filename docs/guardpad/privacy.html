You're in Control

GuardPad is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your scan results and history remain on your device.

Data Collection

GuardPad does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers. Scan results, history, and settings remain exclusively on your device.

Data Storage

Your scan history is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. No personal information is collected during scans.

Network Requests

When you scan a URL, your device connects directly to the target server to retrieve HTTP headers and certificate information. No intermediary servers are used.

DNS record lookups use DNS-over-HTTPS via the public Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) and Google (8.8.8.8) resolvers. These are standard DNS queries — no personal data is included beyond the domain name you choose to look up.

Third-Party Services

GuardPad does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only network requests are the security scans you initiate.

We use Apple's App Store for distribution and purchase management. Apple's privacy policy governs their handling of purchase data.

Feedback & Support Email

Feedback is always voluntary. GuardPad shows you the full message before anything is shared, and it is only sent if you send it yourself from your Mail app (or copy it into an email). The message contains what you write plus the app version, build and iOS version. A finding type, the scanned domain or the observed header value are only included if you choose to. Cookies, authorization headers, query strings and URL credentials are never included.

Messages you send arrive in the support inbox (op.apps@outlook.com) and are used only to answer you and improve GuardPad.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Export scan reports at any time
  • Delete individual scans or all history
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app

Children's Privacy

GuardPad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
