Understand, fix, verify

Built for freelance developers and small web agencies who look after client sites.

📊

1 · Understand

An A+ to F grade that summarises the checks performed, with the highest-impact fixes first. A grade is not a guarantee of security or a certification, and GuardPad says so.

🔧

2 · Fix

Every finding shows what was observed, why it matters and what to change. PRO adds ready-to-paste config for nginx, Apache, Express.js and Caddy.

✅

3 · Verify

Rescan the same site. Each earlier finding is marked Resolved, Still present, New or Not verified. An unchanged overall grade never hides a fix, and an incomplete scan never counts as one.

🔔

Follow up without guessing

Pin client sites. When iOS allows, GuardPad re-scans them in the background and notifies you only when a grade or header changes. Each site shows when it was last checked — and whether that check failed or is getting old. PRO.

🔒

What is checked

11 graded security headers, SSL/TLS including post-quantum key exchange, cookie flags, SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MTA-STS and BIMI, redirects, security.txt and WAF/CDN fingerprints — mapped to OWASP Top 10:2025.

📱

Private by design

No account. No analytics SDKs. No tracking. Scans run from your device and results stay on it. Feedback is optional — you see the whole message first and send it yourself.

Honest monitoring status

Each pinned domain shows when it was last checked in the background, whether the last check failed, and when the information may be out of date. You're told if Background App Refresh or notifications are off.

Feedback you control

"Report an incorrect result" on findings, and "Send Feedback / Request a Feature" in Settings. You preview everything before you send it from Mail — nothing is sent automatically.

Scans of different sites are no longer compared, the SSL badge shows the direction of change, Restore Purchases syncs with the App Store first, and the largest text sizes no longer cut off the scan field.

PRO

Yearly or Lifetime
