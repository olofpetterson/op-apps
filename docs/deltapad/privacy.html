You're in Control

DeltaPad does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and what you paste, type or share is processed on your device.

Your Content

Text you enter into a tool is kept in memory while the app is open so you can switch between tools. It is not saved to disk and is gone when the app closes.

What Is Stored on Your Device

Your preferences, pinned tools, recently used tools, recent UUIDs and, if you use PRO, your ten most recent generated passwords are stored on your device using Apple's standard app storage. They are not synced or uploaded.

DeltaPad shares a small amount of data between the app, its widgets and its share extension on your device, for example the latest UUID and which tools you used recently. Text you share to DeltaPad from another app is passed to the app on your device and is not kept.

What Uses the Network

If you buy or restore DeltaPad PRO, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Nothing else in the app uses the network. The tools work offline, and Markdown Preview does not load images or other content from the internet. If you tap a link, it opens in your browser.

DeltaPad contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, creates no profiles, and does not sync your data to iCloud.

Your Control

Everything DeltaPad stores is on your device, so you decide what happens to it:

  • Copy or share results at any time
  • Clear your recent passwords in Password Generator
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app

App Store Ratings

DeltaPad may ask the system to show Apple's rating prompt after you have used the app several times. The decision about when to ask is made on your device, and Apple decides whether the prompt is shown.

JWT Decoding

The JWT Decoder decodes tokens on your device. It does not verify signatures and does not send tokens anywhere.

This Website

This website counts page views with GoatCounter, which does not use cookies. The app itself contains no analytics.

Children's Privacy

DeltaPad is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
