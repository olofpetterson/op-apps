Troubleshooting

🔒

The Full Campaign is still locked

Connect to the internet, check that you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with, and tap Restore purchases in Settings. If Ask to Buy is on, the purchase unlocks once it has been approved.

🔈

No music or sound

Check that Music and Sound effects are on in Settings and that the volume on your iPhone is turned up.

📱

The game stutters or closes

Close other apps and restart your iPhone. If it keeps happening, email us your iPhone model, iOS version and the mission where it happened.
