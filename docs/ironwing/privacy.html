No Ads

Ironwing collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your campaign, settings and play statistics are stored only on your iPhone.

Data Collection

Ironwing does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

Data Stored on Your Device

To keep your game, Ironwing stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Campaign progress, unlocked aircraft, upgrades, liveries and challenge results
  • Settings: music, sound effects, haptics, reduced flashing and difficulty
  • Play statistics and high scores

This data never leaves your device, except as part of the device backup you choose to make through iCloud or your computer. Ironwing has no cloud sync of its own.

Purchases

The Full Campaign is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never receive your name, email address or payment details. Ironwing only checks with the App Store whether the Full Campaign belongs to your Apple Account, so it can unlock or restore it.

Third-Party Services

Ironwing does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency because it never tracks you.

We use Apple's App Store for distribution and purchases. Apple's privacy policy governs their handling of purchase data.

Your Rights

Since everything stays on your device, you are in full control:

  • Change sound, haptics, flashing and difficulty at any time in Settings
  • Delete all game data by deleting the app
  • Restore the Full Campaign at any time with Restore purchases

Children's Privacy

Ironwing collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date.
