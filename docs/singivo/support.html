Troubleshooting

🎙

Singivo cannot hear me

Check that Singivo is allowed to use the microphone: open the Settings app on your iPhone, then Apps, Singivo, and turn on Microphone. A button in Settings in Singivo takes you there.

🔈

It says the sound is unclear

Practise in a quiet room, hold the iPhone at about arm's length or closer, and hum or sing one steady sound. Very quiet, breathy or creaky sounds are hard to measure, and an unclear try is never counted as a miss.

🛒

The Premium screen says the App Store couldn't be reached

Check your internet connection and try again. Prices and purchases come from the App Store, so the free course works offline but buying does not.

⏳

My purchase is waiting for approval

With Ask to Buy, a family organiser has to approve the purchase. Premium opens by itself once it is approved.
