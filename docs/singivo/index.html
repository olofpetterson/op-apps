A patient first step

For adults who have never been sure they can hold a note.

🎵

Your own note

You start by humming. Singivo suggests a note that suits your voice, and you can move it if it feels too high or too low.

💬

Plain-words feedback

After each try you see whether you were on the note, a little low or a little high, and what to try next.

⏱

Short lessons

Hear the note, sing it back, try again. A lesson is five or six attempts and takes a few minutes.

👁

With and without the graph

Some lessons show your pitch while you sing. Others hide it, so you find out what you can do by ear.

📅

A check you can repeat

The last lesson is a short check without help. Do it again another day and compare the two results.

🔒

Private by design

Your voice is analysed on your iPhone. No audio is saved or uploaded, and no account is needed.

What is in the app

One complete free course, and one optional paid level.

Level 0: Discover Your Voice (free)

Eight lessons, free, without ads and without limits: hearing high and low, finding your first note, approaching a note from below and from above, holding a note, singing from memory, telling pitch from loudness, and a check you can repeat on another day.

Level 1: Sing in Tune (Singivo Premium)

An optional subscription opens five more lessons: a narrower target, starting on the note, longer holds, stepwise notes and a level check. Further levels are planned and are not part of the subscription until they are released. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Singivo is a practice aid, not a singing teacher or medical advice. Stop if singing hurts or feels strained.
