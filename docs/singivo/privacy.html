No account

This policy covers the Singivo app for iPhone and this website. Singivo is published by Olof Petterson. In short: the app has no account, no advertising and no analytics, and nothing you sing leaves your iPhone.

Microphone

Singivo uses the microphone only while you hum or sing in a lesson. The sound is analysed on your iPhone to measure pitch, and is then discarded. No audio is saved, and no audio is sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your iPhone

To show your history and suggest what to practise next, Singivo saves the following on your iPhone:

  • Which lessons you did and when
  • Results of each attempt: the note you aimed for, how far from it you were, and how much clear sound was measured
  • Your answers in the listening lessons
  • Your settings, such as language

We cannot see this data. It may be included in the backups of your iPhone that you make with iCloud or a computer, under Apple's terms.

Optional study log

Singivo has a study log that is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When on, the app keeps a local list of practice milestones together with a random installation number. It contains no audio, name or contact details. It is never sent automatically: it leaves your iPhone only if you choose to share the file yourself, after seeing exactly what it contains. Entries older than 90 days are removed, and you can delete the log at any time in Settings.

Subscriptions

Singivo Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks the App Store on your iPhone whether a subscription is active, and that is the only time the app uses the internet. Apple gives us sales reports that do not identify you. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking, and it does not share data with any third party.

This website

This website counts page visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not store information that identifies you. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to answer you. Ask us and we will delete the correspondence.

Your choices

Because your practice data exists only on your iPhone, you control it:

  • Delete all practice history in Settings in Singivo
  • Delete the study log in Settings in Singivo
  • Turn off microphone access in the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Remove everything by deleting the app

Children's Privacy

Singivo is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the date at the top will change.
